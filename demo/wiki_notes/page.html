A text arrives

These are simulated rider texts. Names, a phone number, and an email are still in them. Nothing has been stored.

Slide 2 · agents/community.py

The message row

The community agent stores each text in SQLite. The sender becomes a rider- id. Phone numbers and emails are removed before the row is written.

Slide 3 · ingestion/claims.py

The claim rows

Rules split each stored message into claims. The wiki is allowed to read these rows. It never reads the raw text above.

Slide 4 · backend/distill.py · backend/testimonies.py

Place notes

The wiki reads the claim rows and files a note on the place it names. A question stays in the database and does not become a note.

Slide 5 · backend/distill.py topic_digest

Topic pages

The same claims are clustered into the topic pages the site serves: a summary, the clearest tip, and the latest update. Haiku is not in this path. It writes a route brief later, from notes that already exist.
